# Data-Mesh Concepts in TokMesh Lite

## Data product

A data product is a named, owned, versioned offering with documented data, expectations, and permitted use. A file alone is not a data product.

## Contract

The manifest is the first TokMesh contract. It states the schema, required values, quality expectations, owner, classification, and allowed consumers.

## Owner

The owner is accountable for the product's meaning and quality. TokMesh Lite records a country and organization; later stages will model richer stewardship.

## Quality

Quality rules make expectations executable. Lite begins with required values, uniqueness, and numeric ranges.

## Sovereignty

The sovereignty section records classification and allowed countries. Lite validates the declaration but does not enforce remote access; federation stages will do that.

## Publication readiness

A product is ready for publication only when its manifest is internally valid and its dataset satisfies the declared contract.
